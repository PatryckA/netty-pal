Netty Pal privacy

Last updated 28 September 2026. Back to Netty Pal

Netty Pal is a small web app that helps junior netball teams plan lineups, track court time and score games. This page explains what information it uses and how to have it removed.

What we collect

  • Your sign-in details: your name, email address and profile photo (if you have one) from Google or Facebook, or the email and name you type when signing in by email. These let your team see who's who.
  • Team information added by coaches and parents: team and player names, fixtures, lineups, scores, goals, misses and court time.

We don't collect payment details, your contacts, or your location. If you use voice scoring, your phone's own speech service (Google or Apple) turns speech into text; Netty Pal doesn't record or store audio.

How it's used

Only to run the app for your team. Information is visible to the members of the team you join. It isn't sold, shared with advertisers, or used for anything else.

Where it's stored

In Google Firebase (Google Cloud), which handles sign-in and stores the team data securely.

Deleting your information

To leave a team, open it in Netty Pal, go to the Helpers tab and tap Leave this season. A team admin can also remove you.

To have your account and everything linked to it deleted (including if you signed in with Facebook), email patryck.allen@gmail.com from the address you sign in with, with the subject "Netty Pal data deletion". It will be deleted within 30 days and you'll get a reply when it's done.
